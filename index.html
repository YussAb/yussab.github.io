Problems worth solving

These are themes and opportunities I find interesting. They are meant to engage collaborators, founders, and investors.

01

Cognitive health in the age of AI

As screen time and the use of AI increase, people experience more difficulty focusing, remembering information, and managing mental fatigue.

There are still few tools that help individuals understand and control how technology affects their cognitive health.

02

The healthy living gap

People know what it means to live healthy, but struggle to act on it consistently.

Daily routines, limited time, and competing priorities make healthy behaviors difficult to sustain.

03

The hidden value of older adults

Older adults represent a growing and often overlooked resource in society.

Their experience, knowledge, and available time are still underused because there are limited systems that enable them to actively contribute.
